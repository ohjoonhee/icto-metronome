Support

Help for Icto Metronome on iPhone.

Contact

To report a problem or ask a question, open an issue on GitHub. Please say which iPhone and which version of iOS you use, and what you did before the problem appeared.

Common questions

Which iPhones does it work on?
Any iPhone with iOS 26 or later.

I hear nothing.
The click plays even when the silent switch is on, so check the volume buttons first, and check whether the sound is going to headphones or a speaker you connected earlier.

Does it stop my music or video?
No. The click plays over the sound of other apps and does not pause them.

Does it keep playing when the screen is locked?
Yes. While it plays, the tempo is shown on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island, where you can also stop it.

Where are my songs and settings kept?
On your iPhone only. Deleting the app deletes them. See the Privacy Policy.
