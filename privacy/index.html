Icto Metronome

Privacy Policy

Effective November 23, 2026

Icto Metronome does not collect any data about you.

What the app does not do

  • It has no accounts and asks for no personal information.
  • It does not connect to the internet. Nothing you do in the app is sent to the developer or to anyone else.
  • It contains no advertising, no analytics, and no third-party code that tracks you.
  • It does not use the microphone, the camera, your location, your contacts, or your photos.

What stays on your iPhone

Your settings and the songs you create are saved on your iPhone only, so the app opens the way you left it. Deleting the app deletes them.

While the metronome plays, the tempo can appear on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island. iOS shows this on your device; it is not sent anywhere.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the support page.
